## Project Overview
This project investigates the relationship between NFL team winning percentages and home-game attendance. Our goal is to determine whether more successful NFL teams consistently attract higher attendance and whether there is a statistically significant correlation between team performance and fan attendance.

## Research Question
Is there a significant correlation between NFL teams' winning percentages and their home-game attendance rates?

## Datasets
1. NFL Stadium Attendance Dataset
   - Source: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/sujaykapadnis/nfl-stadium-attendance-dataset
   - Years: 2000–2019
   - Includes NFL attendance by team, season, and week.

2. NFL Team Data 2003–2023
   - Source: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/nickcantalupa/nfl-team-data-2003-2023
   - Years: 2003–2023
   - Includes team-level performance statistics and winning percentages.

The overlapping period is 2003–2019.

## Project Plan
1. Import both datasets into R.
2. Clean and standardize team names, seasons, and relevant variables.
3. Join the datasets by team and season.
4. Calculate season-level home attendance metrics and summarize winning percentages.
5. Explore the relationship through tables, graphics, and statistical inference.

## Planned Visualizations
- Distribution of home-game attendance rates.
- Scatterplot of team winning percentage versus attendance, with a fitted regression line.
- Team-by-season heatmaps comparing attendance and winning percentages.

## Statistical Analysis
Null hypothesis (H0): There is no linear correlation between team winning percentage and home attendance (rho = 0).

Alternative hypothesis (H1): There is a nonzero linear correlation (rho != 0).

We plan to estimate the Pearson correlation coefficient, conduct a two-sided correlation test, and report a 95% confidence interval.
